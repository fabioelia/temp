Statement: COPD risk from HHIP-expression-lowering alleles at 4q31 acts partly
through ongoing adult mesenchymal Hedgehog activity, so SMO inhibition should
help HHIP risk-allele carriers.

PREDICTION IF TRUE:
 (a) HHIP has strong direct genetic evidence for COPD in Open Targets (genetic
     association datatype dominant, score high, top-ranked COPD target);
 (b) SMO/GLI1/PTCH1 also carry at least some independent human genetic signal
     for COPD or lung function (association score > ~0.1 with a genetic
     component), i.e. the pathway, not just the one gene;
 (c) SMO, GLI1, PTCH1 are robustly expressed in adult GTEx lung (TPM well
     above noise) and ideally have lung eQTLs -> a druggable adult target;
 (d) HHIP GWAS associations include progression / FEV1-decline / emphysema
     phenotypes, not only cross-sectional baseline lung function;
 (e) SMO is high-tractability with approved small molecules (repurposable).

PREDICTION IF FALSE:
 (a) HHIP genetic evidence is real but SMO/GLI1/PTCH1 have no COPD genetic
     evidence -> the "pathway" claim rests on inference, not genetics;
 (b) Hh effectors poorly expressed in adult lung;
 (c) all HHIP GWAS hits are cross-sectional baseline FEV1/FEV1-FVC/COPD
     case-control, with no longitudinal decline signal -> the developmental
     (lung-growth) explanation is not separable from the ongoing-adult-
     signalling explanation using public summary data, so the decisive test
     is NOT runnable -> hypothesis unfalsifiable from this layer;
 (d) no SMO inhibitor trial in COPD/emphysema; SMO inhibitors approved only
     for basal cell carcinoma / AML, with known tolerability burden.

DIRECTIONALITY NOTE (kill criterion): HHIP risk alleles LOWER HHIP expression.
HHIP is an inhibitor of HH ligands -> less HHIP = MORE Hh signalling. An SMO
inhibitor would therefore be mechanistically coherent ONLY if excess adult Hh
activity is pathogenic. If published lung biology instead shows HH signalling
is required for adult alveolar maintenance (i.e. Hh loss causes emphysema),
SMO inhibition would be predicted to HARM, not help -> falsifies.

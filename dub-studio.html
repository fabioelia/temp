2 · Languages

Claude translates whole scenes at once, so lines keep their tone and fit the time each character has to speak. Only the transcript text is sent to the Anthropic API; audio and video stay on this machine.

3 · Voices

How loud the original track stays while a dubbed line plays. Music and effects are kept.

4 · Dub

Live dubbing runs about 1–3 s behind the speaker. For the best result, prepare a full dub, which also lets you edit lines and voices before you watch.

5 · Export

Mux the WAV with the original video using:
ffmpeg -i episode.mp4 -i dub.wav -filter_complex "[0:a]volume=0.25[o];[o][1:a]amix=inputs=2:normalize=0" -map 0:v -c:v copy episode-dubbed.mp4

2 · Languages

4 · Dub

Live dubbing runs about 1–3 s behind the speaker. For the best result, prepare a full dub, which also lets you edit lines and voices before you watch.

5 · Export

Put the full mix on the original picture with:
ffmpeg -i episode.mp4 -i episode.mix.wav -map 0:v -map 1:a -c:v copy -c:a aac episode-dubbed.mp4

Voice Lab · train a voice from your talent

Train a custom Kokoro voice from recordings of a voice actor, entirely on this device (WebGPU on Apple Silicon). Each voice records the talent's release reference, which travels with the voice when it is exported. Trained voices speak English.

1 · Talent and recordings

1–5 minutes of clean, solo speech works best: no music, no other voices.

2 · Release on file

Saved with the voice and included in its exports.

3 · Train

Match score is the voice-print similarity to the talent (1.0 = identical).

**Execution rule that drives everything:** order decided after close D fills at close D+1. For an after-close (AMC) report on day R, deciding the evening of R-1 means holding THROUGH the gap, which is a gamble. Deciding the evening of R fills at R+1 close, after the gap, which is a post-event trade. A before-open (BMO) report is always post-event unless you decide two evenings ahead.

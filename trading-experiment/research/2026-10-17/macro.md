## 1. Verdict
Not a no-trade zone, but a pay-to-wait week. This is a **late-cycle correction inside a bull, driven by liquidity and credit, not earnings**, and every ingredient for something worse is present. Doubling in 23 days is roughly a 4% proposition on any strategy. Deploy at most 40% on Monday and hold the rest until the AI prints and the Oct 28 FOMC.

**Reflexivity:** AI capex is increasingly debt-funded. Falling equities raise funding costs, which widen CRWV/ORCL spreads, which cut capex, which cut semis estimates. Credit leads equity, and the S&P hasn't priced it. An Oracle junk downgrade (forced fallen-angel selling) is the accelerant. With weak jobs and a crowded Treasury short, the pain trade is a bond rally.

## 2. Lean and avoid
- **Lean:** Energy is the only sector with rising revisions and it hedges the stagflation tail. Cash is a position until Oct 28.
- **Avoid:** Debt-funded AI infrastructure (CRWV, ORCL, neoclouds), memory/semis knife-catching before credit stabilizes, rate-sensitives (REITs, utilities, Russell 2000 -3% on the week), single names through binary prints.
- **Oil asymmetry:** A Hormuz deal can take 10-15% off crude in days (April precedent), and the White House wants lower pump prices before Nov 3. Size energy accordingly.

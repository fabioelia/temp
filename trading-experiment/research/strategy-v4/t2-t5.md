**Why T4 and T5 are not evidence.** The universe is today's S&P 100 (Wikipedia, as of 2026-09-21; GOOG dropped), so every past month picks among future survivors. A placebo, labelled post-hoc, holds the five *worst*-ranked names, and they also beat SPY: by 0.96%/month for T5 and 0.66% for T4. The signal's own part, top 5 minus bottom 5, is not significant in either half:
* T5: +0.11%/month (t 0.10) / +1.32% (t 1.79).
* T4: +0.48% (t 0.60) / +0.88% (t 1.88).

The pre-specified excess over an equal-weight basket of the same survivors fails t 2 before 2010 (T5 1.12, T4 1.40). The 28-30% CAGRs are survivorship, so the dollar figures are upper bounds.

**What follows**
* All four clear probe admission: WEAK or better, with the right sign in both halves.
* None should graduate. Before any vote on T4 or T5, require a test with point-in-time membership, for example Ken French's momentum deciles.
* T3 is the cleanest small effect: Sharpe +0.10 in both halves, about +$27 a year as a sleeve. Its drawdown benefit disappeared after 2015.
* T2 lowers drawdown by under 1 point. It saved TLT in 2022 (+1.4% vs -31.2%) but cost GLD 0.26 of Sharpe after 2015.

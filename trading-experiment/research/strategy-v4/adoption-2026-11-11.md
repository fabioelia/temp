* **Admission:** a registered claim, a committed script, a verdict of WEAK or better, a "who is on the other side" line, stocks or ETFs only, held
  overnight (PDT), no S&P 500 fund, no SPY before 12/06.
* **Live probes:**
  - **PR1, gold sleeve (T3 WEAK):** $50 of GLDM (gold, 0.10% fee), held. Checkpoints at weeks 4, 8 and 12 (12/9, 1/6, 2/3).
  - **PR2, non-equity trend (T2 WEAK):** $50 split equally among GLD, TLT and DBC, only those whose 12-month total return beats T-bills at the month-end
    check (the rest in SGOV). Rebalanced at the first pre-close check of each month; the same checkpoints.
  - **PR3, earnings run-up in shares (T1 CONFIRMED vs SPY, WEAK vs random entry):** $25 each in up to 2 of the 25 C1 names, bought at the close 7 sessions
    before the earnings date and sold at the close the session before the print. It starts with the next eligible event (mid-December names).
  - **PR4:** open for the next WEAK-or-better idea.
* **Paper probes (shadow book):** V3 (the volatility-managed core: monthly paper weights), V1 (C1 on the 76 extra names), and W2 flag days.
* **Kill** (checked only at the week 4, 8 and 12 checkpoints, except a -$25 hard stop on any single-stock probe): P/L below its pre-computed band; fill
  costs above 2x the model; signal rate off by 3x; any unregistered action; or week 12 without the backtest upgraded to CONFIRMED.
* **Graduate** (all of): CONFIRMED under PROTOCOL; a deflated Sharpe of at least 0.95 across the whole register; 12 weeks and 10 live signals inside the
  bands. Then it is scaled in steps.
* **Honest limit:** $50 probes cannot show an edge (P/L noise is about 10x the signal). They test fills, costs, signal rates and discipline; backtests
  decide edges.
* **Position limit:** 4 becomes 8 tickers (core 2, probes 4 with PR2 counting as one, options 2).

## B. Policy-event watch (adopted)
* **W0:** before any scheduled election, major bill or ruling within 10 sessions, Run B writes a "who loses" basket (sectors and stocks, with SPY weight)
  from published notes.
* **W1:** policy-specific prediction markets are added to `predmkt.py`'s watch list when such an event is pending; odds that double or move 10 points in
  30 days are flagged.
* **W2, divergence (P1 WEAK, so a caution rule, not a block):** on an event day plus 2 sessions, if SPY is up while XLK lags by 1 point or more, SOXX by
  2.5 points or more, or the basket falls 2% or more, a new bullish index option is half-sized and held calls get the mid-session stop check.
* **W4:** after an event that resolves after the close, Run 0 records NQ=F against ES=F and flags a gap of 1 point or more.
* W3 (VXN/VIX) is not adopted: untested.

## C. Research cadence (adopted)
The full persona round in Run B runs only when, within 2 sessions, there is a C1 reaction day, a PR3 entry or exit, an open-option or probe decision,
or a scheduled policy event; and always at the Sunday review. Other nights get a single news and filings check by the orchestrator. The evidence: 12
rounds from 11/02 to 11/10 produced no trades.

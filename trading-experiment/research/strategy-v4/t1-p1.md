## T1: earnings premium in shares: **CONFIRMED by the claims.md rule; the earnings-specific part is WEAK**

`python3 -I t1_earnings_shares.py <C2 cache>` uses C2's events and committed code. Each event is a buy at the close t-7 and a sale at the close t-1, minus 10 bps.

| Period | n | Net excess vs SPY | t (month-clustered) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full | 2,260 | +0.42% | 4.20 |
| 2005-09 | 474 | +0.62% | 2.24 |
| 2010-26 | 1,786 | +0.37% | 3.57 |
| 2015-26 | 1,254 | +0.40% | 3.08 |

Hit rate 55%; worst event -32.5%.

About 0.26 points of the gross +0.52% is the names' ordinary drift against SPY. That drift reflects survivorship: these are today's mega-caps. Against PROTOCOL's random-entry baseline the net edge is +0.16% (t 1.68): +0.13% (t 1.30) in 2010-26 and +0.11% (t 0.88) since 2015. The earnings-only variant gives the same result. **Probe-admissible; far from the DSR bar (about t 3.7).**

## P1: election divergence gate: **FAIL (label WEAK)**

`python3 -I p1_divergence.py <p1_yahoo dir>` counts SPY +1% days on which XLK, XLF, XLV or XLY lagged SPY by at least 2 of its own 63-day daily SDs. The outcome is SPY's 5-day minimum at -3% or worse. VIX terciles are cut per sample. The test is a Mantel-Haenszel risk ratio with a stratified one-sided Fisher exact test.

| Sample | Flagged | Other +1% days | RR | p |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1999-03 to 2012 | 6/11 (55%) | 115/544 (21%) | 2.09 | 0.043 |
| **2013-2026** | **2/12 (17%)** | **61/419 (15%)** | **1.22** | **0.517** |

Out of sample it fails both the 1.5x bar and p < 0.10, so **W2 is now a sizing check only**. The declared first-in-cluster sensitivity also fails (RR 2.77, p 0.16, on 5 flags). Adding SOXX changes nothing.

XLK never flagged out of sample; 10 of the 12 flags were XLV or XLF. The gate would not have fired on 11/4/2026 (XLK lagged 1.1 SDs, SOXX 1.8).

## Deviations

1. **P1 sector weights.** I could not source point-in-time SPY weights, so I fixed the sector set. I chose XLK, XLF, XLV and XLY because they are the only SPDR sectors near or above 10% for most of 1998-2026. This rests on recalled weights, not a source. XLC starts in 2018 at about 8-11%; XLE was above 10% only around 2005-11.
2. **P1 start date.** The sector ETFs start in 12/1998, so the in-sample period begins in 1999-03.
3. **P1 p-values.** Overlapping, clustered +1% days make the p-values anti-conservative.
4. **T1 data.** I fetched no new T1 data; the C2 cache ends 10/23.

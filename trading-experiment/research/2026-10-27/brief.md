* **Options only, buying only:** buy-to-open calls or puts on liquid US stocks or ETFs, sell to close later. No selling to open, no spreads, no exercise, no stock buys.
* **Sizing:** premium comes only from cash above the $1,000 floor. Shots of about $250-400, never above $450 in one order. At most two shots open, one new shot a day,
  and at least 2 contracts when the price allows, so one can be banked.
* **Contract:** 11/20 or another expiry two to five weeks out (never under 3 days to expiry), delta about 0.30-0.45. Open interest at least 1,000 at the strike; the
  spread at most 5% of the mid (10% for USO-type ETFs). XLE options often fail the spread rule.
* **Consensus check:** say what a generic AI would recommend and whether your idea matches it. A match needs one measured edge, with its figure stated:

  - or IV at or below 20-day realized;
  - or a base rate translated into the option's own payoff odds with EV above 1.
* **Exits decided at entry:**

  - Stops: the thesis stop is a level in the underlying, with a -50% premium stop as backstop. Once a 14:43 check shows +40%, the stop moves to cost.

  - Triggers are checked once a day at about 14:43 ET on live quotes, and fills happen at that day's close. On 10/28 the check is at about 15:30, after the Fed press conference.

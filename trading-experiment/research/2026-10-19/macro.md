## The 10y at 5.15% with stocks up
- **Treasury curve:** the 10y is 5.15% (+6bp), the 2y 4.77% (+3bp) and the TIPS real 10y 2.80% (+6bp).
  - So the 10y breakeven is flat at 2.35%, and it has held between 2.20% and 2.40% for 60 days, including Brent's run from 100.20 (10/7) to 109.86 (10/16).

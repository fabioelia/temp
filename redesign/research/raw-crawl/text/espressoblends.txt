Extra Bar
The coffee of choice for the Italian “bar”. Very popular among Italian coffee shops with unforgettable taste.  A blend that continues to be refined over the years to ensure it’s spot in the class of “authentic” Italian espresso.
5 Stelle
A blend of carefully selected Central-American, Brazilian Arabica and other top quality beans.
Espresso Bar
Central and South American select beans blended and toasted to perfection for excellent cream texture and unforgettable taste.  A blend that has gained respect and popularity across the globe.
Espresso Classico
South American and African Robusta and Arabica beans result in a strong coffee.  A favorite in many homes and bars alike.  The blend that started the Guglielmo tradition.
Caffetteria
Combination of South American, African and Asian beans renowned for intense flavor, aroma and smooth crema.
Copacabana
All around balanced blend ideal for cappuccinos, adding kick to other blends but also for a robust espresso of its own.

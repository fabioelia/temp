Machine Maintenance
After Each Use
(1 ) Rinse and wipe portafilter then replace onto grouphead.
(2) Purge the steam wand before each drink, wipe it with a clean towel and purge again after frothing milk.
*(3) Wipe grouphead screen as needed
Daily Maintenance
(3) Use a small amount of espresso group cleaner and back wash each group 4-6  times for 20-30 seconds.  Finish by thoroughly rinsing the group with water. Remember to make a sacrificial espresso shot every morning, especially after a cleaning.
(4) Remove baskets from portafilters and soak both in espresso cleaning solution for at least an hour, rinse and replace portafilters in groupheads overnight.
Weekly Maintenance
(3) Use a small amount of espresso group cleaner and back wash each group 4-6  times for 10-20 seconds.  Finish by thoroughly rinsing the group with water
(4) Remove baskets from portafilters and soak both in espresso cleaning solution for at least an hour, rinse and replace portafilters in groupheads overnight.
(3) If possible remove shower screens and dispersion block and soak in espresso group cleaner for at least an hour, rinse and replace.
(5) Remove coffee beans from hopper and clean  hopper with warm soapy water, dry and replace. Its good practice to vacuum grinder burrs at this point as well.
Long Term Maintenance (4 - 6 Months)
(6) Replace grouphead gaskets to prevent leaks and a drop in group pressure.
(7) Replace any filter/water softener cartridges and (if separate) regenerate/replace water softener resin

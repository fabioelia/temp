# Five creative directions

All five share the required page set and global features in `concept-brief-base.md`. They differ in strategic bet,
information architecture emphasis, visual language and signature features, so the client can choose a direction
(or mix: e.g. the brand system of one with the B2B tooling of another).

| # | Name | Strategic bet | Lead audience | Signature features |
|---|------|---------------|---------------|--------------------|
| 01 | **Centro** | Turn the bullseye into a bold, recognisable pop-modern brand system and sell direct | Home espresso drinkers, gifting, younger buyers | "Hit your mark" coffee-finder quiz, intensity rings, subscribe & save |
| 02 | **Calabria** | Premium origin storytelling that justifies price and builds love, commerce woven into stories | Discerning home buyers, Italian-American loyalists, press | Tasting Room (blend comparison + sensory profiles), Il Giornale magazine + long-form article |
| 03 | **Pro** | B2B-first: win cafés, restaurants, offices, hotels and distributors; make reordering effortless | Business owners, office managers, distributors | Solution Finder with cost-per-cup and margin math, trade portal (reorder, invoices, service), service hub |
| 04 | **Crema** | Make single-serve systems and machines simple and desirable; sell bundles | Machine buyers, capsule/pod owners | System chooser (36 mm / Nespresso-compatible / ESE / traditional), bundle builder with cost per shot and savings |
| 05 | **Quartiere** | Own the local story: Boston's Italian coffee family, the cafés that pour it, loyalty and referral | Greater Boston locals, Italian-American community, local venues | Neighbourhood map stories, "Il Passaporto" loyalty + referral, recipes |

## 01 — Centro (Italian pop modernism, the bullseye brand system)
- **Thesis:** Guglielmo owns one of the most graphic marks in coffee. Build a whole system from it: concentric rings, flat
  primaries, poster-scale type, the "hit the mark" idea in copy and interaction. Confident and joyful, after 1950s–60s Italian
  advertising posters (not retro pastiche: modern and crisp).
- **IA:** Coffee · Capsules & Machines · Find your blend · For Business · Our Story (+ Find us in utility bar).
- **Visual:** flat brand primaries (red #ED1C24, orange #F5821F, yellow #FFF200, reserved for highlights on dark), ink black,
  clean white; each blend gets its packaging color as an identity color; huge numerals; concentric ring motifs as section
  dividers, intensity meters and buttons; color-block bands; bold cut-out packshots on color fields.
- **Type direction:** a muscular grotesk with width range for poster headlines (e.g. Archivo variable at narrow/expanded widths,
  or another characterful grotesk) + a clean, friendly text grotesk.
- **Signature:** `quiz.html` — 5-question coffee finder (how you brew → system; milk or straight; sweet/balanced/bold; caffeine;
  cups per week) that "hits" a recommendation with a bullseye animation, gives a primary + two alternatives, explains why,
  and offers subscribe & save. Intensity shown everywhere as filled rings (1–10).

## 02 — Calabria (editorial heritage, premium storytelling)
- **Thesis:** A Southern Italian roaster with decades of history on the Ionian coast deserves to be told like a magazine.
  Slow, image-forward chapters; commerce placed inside stories ("the blend in this story" modules).
- **IA:** Coffee · Machines · Tasting Room · Il Giornale · For Business · Our Story.
- **Visual:** NOT the generic cream-and-terracotta look. Think Ionian Sea at blue hour and sunlit limestone: deep sea ink, a
  clear Mediterranean blue, chalk white, lemon yellow (echoing the bullseye's yellow ring) and Guglielmo red as a rare accent.
  Asymmetric magazine grids, full-bleed photo chapters, captions with real place names and coordinates (Copanello,
  38.76° N 16.56° E), pull quotes, drop caps, folio numbers.
- **Type direction:** a distinctive high-contrast or Italian-inscriptional display serif, a highly readable text face for
  long-form, and a small utility face for captions/data. Avoid Playfair/Cormorant/Fraunces defaults.
- **Signature:** `tasting.html` — the Tasting Room: every blend with a sensory profile chart (body, crema, sweetness,
  acidity, intensity, bitterness), composition bar (arabica/robusta), "compare two blends" side by side, food pairings.
  `journal.html` — Il Giornale index (6+ real-topic articles), and `article.html` — one fully written long-form piece
  (e.g. "From Copanello to the North End" or "How Calabria takes its coffee") with shoppable blend module.

## 03 — Pro (Guglielmo Professional, B2B-first)
- **Thesis:** Wholesale, equipment and service for New England businesses, plus new distributors nationally, are the growth
  engine. Lead with outcomes (better espresso, less downtime, real margins), make the tasting/quote/reorder flows
  frictionless; consumer shop stays one click away ("Shop for home").
- **IA:** Solutions (by business type) · Equipment · Coffee · Service & Support · Partner with us · Shop for home.
- **Visual:** precise, Swiss/industrial: graphite, steel, white, signal orange for actions (brand orange), red for alerts;
  spec-sheet typography, data tables, crisp product photography on light grey, tabular numerals, modular grid.
- **Type direction:** a utilitarian grotesk with excellent numerals + a monospace or technical companion for data.
- **Signature:** `solutions.html` — Solution Finder wizard (business type, cups/day, milk-drink share, space/plumbing,
  staff experience) → recommended setup (machine class, grinder, blend, capsule vs traditional), monthly coffee estimate,
  cost per cup and margin at your menu price, service plan, illustrative lease-to-own estimate, "Send me this proposal".
  `portal.html` — trade account dashboard demo for a clearly labelled sample business: quick reorder of usual items, standing
  order schedule, invoices, equipment registry with next service date, water-filter reminder, book a technician.
  `service.html` — service & maintenance hub (turn the existing maintenance guide into interactive daily/weekly/6-month
  checklists, brands serviced, book a service form).

## 04 — Crema (immersive dark showroom for systems and machines)
- **Thesis:** Three incompatible single-serve systems confuse buyers today. Make the choice effortless and the ritual
  desirable with cinematic product storytelling, then sell the machine + coffee bundle on cost per shot.
- **IA:** Machines · Capsules & Pods · Espresso Blends · Choose your system · For Business · Story.
- **Visual:** warm dark (espresso, roasted brown) with crema-gradient light, chiaroscuro photography, brand orange/red only
  as tiny precise accents; generous negative space; spec callouts on product images; scroll-linked scenes as progressive
  enhancement. Not "near-black + neon": it should feel like candlelit Italian bar wood and crema.
- **Type direction:** an elegant, slightly unusual display face + a precise sans; numbers matter (cost per shot).
- **Signature:** `systems.html` — "Choose your system": interactive comparison of 36 mm capsules, Espresso 70
  (Nespresso®-compatible), ESE pods, traditional beans; preference filters (milk drinks, budget, counter space, cups/day) →
  animated recommendation with compatible machines and coffees. `bundle.html` — bundle builder: pick machine → capsule
  packs → live cost-per-shot, payback vs café latte, yearly savings chart → add bundle to cart. A generative canvas
  "crema" hero (respects reduced motion).

## 05 — Quartiere (Boston's Italian coffee family, community-first)
- **Thesis:** A family-run Boston importer whose coffee is poured from the North End to East Boston, delivered within three
  business days and serviced in person. Build the brand around people and places, make "find it / bring it to your venue"
  central, and retain the Italian-American core with loyalty and referral.
- **IA:** Shop · Find us · Recipes · Il Passaporto · For your business · Our family.
- **Visual:** neighbourhood warmth without kitsch: café awnings, sign-painter lettering, Calabrian majolica tile patterns
  generated in SVG/canvas from the brand colors, postcard and stamp motifs; a deep green counter-color alongside the brand
  primaries; photography of streets, tables, families.
- **Type direction:** a sign-painted/shopfront display face with personality + a friendly, very readable text face.
- **Signature:** `community.html` — Il Quartiere: map-led stories of the venues that pour Guglielmo (using the real venue
  list), "Bring Guglielmo to your venue". `rewards.html` — Il Passaporto: stamp-card loyalty UI, tiers and referral
  ("Give $10, get $10", labelled as a proposal), newsletter. `recipes.html` — Italian coffee recipes (moka method, caffè
  shakerato, affogato, granita al caffè, tiramisù, Brasilena float) with the blend to use.

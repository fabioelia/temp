# Guglielmo USA — Digital Strategy (agency synthesis)

Inputs: our crawl of lespressousa.com (`research/raw-crawl/`), the live UX/performance audit
(`research/current-site-audit.md`), the competitor benchmark of 17 sites (`research/competitors.md`) and the brand pack
(`research/brand-pack.md`).

## 1. The situation in one paragraph
Guglielmo USA sells a genuinely distinctive product (a Calabrian espresso roasted since the 1940s, certified Espresso
Italiano blends, a family importer in Greater Boston since 2000) through a site that looks and works like 2014. On our
52-point feature matrix it scores **11.5**; every benchmark scores between **25.5 and 43** (illy 43, Nespresso 42,
Lavazza 41). The gap is table stakes, not polish: product information, compatibility, filters, subscriptions, reviews,
contact details, SEO and speed. The business side (wholesale, equipment, service, franchising, distributors) is buried
under "Join Us" and ends in long forms.

## 2. Audiences and their jobs
| Audience | What they come to do | What the site must give them |
|---|---|---|
| Italian-American loyalists (core repeat buyers, nationwide) | Re-order "the coffee from home" quickly | Fast reorder, subscription, the right blend name and pack they know, heritage that confirms authenticity |
| Capsule and pod machine owners | Find refills that fit their machine | "Shop by your machine", compatibility on every product, bulk packs, subscription |
| Espresso-curious home buyers | Choose a blend or a machine | Intensity scale, tasting notes, finder quiz, machine + coffee bundles with cost per cup |
| Cafés, restaurants, pastry shops (New England) | Get better espresso, reliable supply and service | Solution finder, tasting request, equipment + lease, service promise, proof (local venues) |
| Offices, hotels, caterers | A simple, low-mess coffee program | Capsule systems, cost per cup, cups/stirrer kits, delivery promise, short lead form |
| Prospective distributors, retailers, franchisees | Evaluate a partnership | Clear programme pages, territory map, what support they get, short application |

## 3. Principles every concept follows
1. **Two clear doors.** A "For Home | For Business" switch in the header (pattern from Lavazza, Faema, Nespresso). Each mode
   has its own primary call to action: *Subscribe & save* (home) and *Book a tasting* (business).
2. **Shop by your machine first.** Compatibility is the number-one question: 36 mm capsules (Espresso Point-type),
   Espresso 70 (Nespresso® Original-compatible), E.S.E. 44 mm pods, traditional (beans/ground, moka, espresso machine),
   drip. Every product card shows Format · Fits · Intensity.
3. **One intensity scale (1–10)** for every blend, with buckets *Mild 1–4 · Balanced 5–7 · Bold 8–10*, plus roast,
   arabica/robusta share, notes and best brew method. Same scale on cards, product pages, quiz and comparison tools.
4. **No dead ends.** Out-of-stock items are hidden by default (toggle to show) or carry "Notify me"; quote-only machines
   say "Request a quote", never $0; the "Order Adjustment" item disappears.
5. **Make the hidden value visible.** Case discounts (5/6/8% at 6/12/18) shown as a tier table; free-shipping progress in the
   cart; machine + capsule "pays for itself" math shown as cost per shot.
6. **Short forms, then conversation.** First contact ≤ 6 fields; solution finder asks 3–4 questions before contact details;
   every business page offers "Book a tasting".
7. **One source of truth for facts.** Founding year, years of experience, number of blends, certifications and address come
   from the brand pack and appear identically everywhere.
8. **Fast and accessible by default.** Performance budget (< 1 MB, < 25 requests on product pages), WCAG 2.2 AA, real alt text,
   meta descriptions and structured data on every template.

## 4. Feature set (priority from the benchmark)
**Launch (must-have):** home/business switch · shop-by-machine + compatibility badges · standard coffee PDP (intensity,
roast, notes, composition, brew methods, cost per cup) · faceted filters · subscribe & save with skip/pause · visible volume
tiers · reviews with schema (collected from real customers) · catalog hygiene · 4-step business solution finder · book a
tasting · service request with triage · Find Guglielmo map · real brand story + full contact/trust block · Apple Pay /
Google Pay / PayPal + free-shipping bar · SEO baseline · working newsletter.

**Phase 2:** coffee-finder quiz · machine + capsule bundles · tasting kit · trade portal (reorder, PO number, invoices) ·
lease calculator · service plans · segment pages with partner stories · Guglielmo Point franchise page · distributor
programme with territory map · brew guides and recipes · machine support hub · office cost-per-cup calculator.

**Phase 3:** loyalty points · referral · barista training bookings · capsule recycling · gift cards and corporate gifting ·
Italian-American care package · video onboarding for machine buyers · repair status · refurbished machines · perfect-serve kit.

The five concepts each prototype the launch set plus a different slice of phases 2–3, so the client can see them working.

## 5. Information architecture (baseline; concepts may vary labels)
- **Utility bar:** For Home | For Business · Find Guglielmo · Trade login · (617) 612-0090 · Free shipping over $59
- **Home mode nav:** Coffee (shop by machine / format / blend, finder, tasting kit) · Machines (capsule & pod, home espresso,
  bundles, care) · Subscribe · Gifts & Italian pantry (cups, Brasilena, candy) · Learn (our story, blends & intensity guide,
  brew guides, recipes, machine support) · Find Guglielmo
- **Business mode nav:** Solutions (cafés, restaurants & pizzerias, offices, hotels & catering, retail) · Coffee programme
  (wholesale blends, tasting, training, perfect serve) · Equipment (WEGA/BFC, capsule systems, grinders, water treatment,
  lease) · Service & support · Partner with us (franchise, distributor, retailer) · Trade login · **Get a proposal**
- **Footer:** Shop · Business · Support · Company · contact block (verified address, phone, email, hours) · trust row ·
  newsletter · legal

## 6. Open items for the client
- Confirm the public business address: the parent's distributor list shows **266 Calvary St, Waltham, MA 02453**; the site
  says Boston. One address must be used across the site, Google Business Profile and schema.
- Confirm founding year and claims to use (parent says 1943 / "over 80 years"; current site says 60 and 70 years).
- Approve proposed commercial policies shown in the demos: 10% subscribe & save, free shipping at $59, "Give $10, get $10"
  referral, tasting kit.
- Supply high-resolution packshots and partner-venue photography; collect real reviews from past customers.

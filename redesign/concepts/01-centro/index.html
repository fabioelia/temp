    Three generations. One target.

    From a Catanzaro shop to your cup.

    Guglielmo Papaleo opened his shop on Corso Mazzini with a single coffee machine. His family still runs the roastery; ours brings it to New England.

    1943

    Guglielmo Papaleo opens his shop and coffee machine in Catanzaro, Calabria.

    1972

    The roastery opens at Copanello, overlooking the Ionian Sea.

    3,000t

    Tonnes of coffee a year, each origin roasted on its own, then blended.

    2000

    Mimmo Elia starts importing Guglielmo to Boston as L’Espresso USA.

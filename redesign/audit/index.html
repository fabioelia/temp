It sells coffee. It does not sell Guglielmo.

A read-only inspection of every public page, the catalog and the checkout with Playwright, axe-core and network throttling. The store works, but search engines can't index its shop, shoppers can't tell what fits their machine, and business buyers can't find the business.

Scorecard

Thirteen areas, scored 1 to 10.

Brand & Storytelling

3/10

70+ years of Italian roasting heritage is buried: the hero is a 137px strip using a 284x300 thumbnail stretched to 1440px with tagline-as-image, About is 3 paragraphs about 'L'Espresso USA', and 'The Story of Guglielmo' has 44 words plus an outbound link.

Visual Design

3/10

Stock 2014 Enfold template: grey 13px text, faint ghost buttons, mixed icon styles, 2014-15 product photos (75 of 156 catalog images) and inconsistent card layouts. It reads as dated and generic, not premium Italian.

Navigation & IA

4/10

The Shop mega-menu is usable, but B2B sits behind the vague label 'Join Us'. 'About' is not clickable, 'Espresso Blends' sits under About, Learn More anchors are wrong, Equipment pages are not in the menu, and the mobile menu is a flat list of 38 links.

Product Discovery

3/10

/shop/ shows only 3 tiles, filters are single-select link lists that sit 7,762px down on mobile, 46% of SKUs are out of stock with no label, and search returns blog-style results with no images or prices.

Product Detail Pages

4/10

The buy box is clean and the case-discount table is a real asset. But the median is 1 image and 54 words of description, there are no reviews or shipping/returns info, an internal 'Equipment Test' option is exposed to buyers, and 'Call for Price' pages have no quote CTA.

Cart & Checkout

5/10

Guest checkout plus PayPal/Venmo/Google Pay on a single page is solid. However, shipping cost is invisible until a full address is entered, 'Apply coupon' is the dominant button in the cart, and there is no express pay on the cart or PDP.

B2B Lead Generation

3/10

Leads go through a 14-field form (9 required) or a 13-field franchise form (9 required, $2.5M net-worth question). There is no phone on the B2B or Contact pages, the distributor path is a PDF, the Distributors tab is empty, and the pages contradict each other.

Mobile

3/10

On 3G with 4x CPU throttling, LCP is 7.2-13.8s and CLS is 0.33 on home. 73% of tap targets on home are under 24px, the median font is 13px, filters sit below the products, and the PDP buy box falls below the fold for returning visitors.

Performance

4/10

Desktop is acceptable (LCP 1.4-2.8s), but every page makes 120-136 requests (~50 CSS + ~55 JS, 56-61 render-blocking). Images are already served as WebP by content negotiation; GTM, Smartlook and Turnstile load sitewide.

Accessibility

3/10

Every audited page has critical axe violations. Contrast failures range from 14 to 102 nodes per page. There is no visible focus indicator, no skip link and 42 header tab stops; CF7 forms have up to 15 unlabeled fields, and 63% of images lack alt text.

SEO

2/10

/shop/ and all 18 categories, 5 tags and 11 brands are noindex (Enfold default; no SEO plugin). robots.txt blocks 52 of 54 CSS/JS files. There are 0 meta descriptions and 0 OG tags, about 41% of sitemap URLs cannot be indexed, and the homepage H1 is 'Front Page'.

Content Quality

3/10

15+ typos and contradictory facts (60 vs 70 years, 20 vs 25 years, 7 vs 8 blends, 6 drip blends claimed vs 3 sold). Also: 2 broken shortcodes, invisible white text, health claims from 2006-2015 and 7 password-protected products published.

Trust & Social Proof

2/10

0 reviews across 87 products and only 2 undated testimonials. No phone, email or address in the header, footer or Contact page; no shipping policy; '© Copyright Guglielmo USA -' with no year. Social links point to the Italian parent page.

CriticalSEO-01

All shop, category, tag and brand archives are noindex

Evidence and fix

<meta name="robots" content="noindex, follow"> on /shop/, all 18 product_cat archives (e.g. /product-category/coffee/, /product-category/coffee/capsules/), 5 product tags and 11 brand pages. No SEO plugin is installed, so Enfold's built-in robots logic applies, and it indexes only single posts and pages. Only 113 URLs are indexable: 26 pages (including /cart/ and /my-account/, which should be noindex) and 87 products. The pages that should rank for 'Guglielmo capsules', 'Italian espresso beans' or 'commercial espresso machine Boston' are therefore excluded from Google.

Fix: Install Rank Math or Yoast (both override Enfold's robots output) and set product categories, the shop page and brand pages to index. Write a unique intro (120-250 words), title and meta description for each key category. Keep attribute archives (pa_*) and tag archives noindex.

CriticalSEO-02

robots.txt blocks rendering resources and every query-string URL; no Sitemap line

Evidence and fix

robots.txt disallows /wp-includes, /wp-content/plugins, /wp-content/themes and '/*?*'. Under Google's longest-match rules, 52 of 54 same-origin CSS/JS files on the homepage (53/54 on a PDP) are blocked for Googlebot, including jquery.min.js?ver=3.7.1 and every Enfold stylesheet. admin-ajax.php and ?wc-ajax endpoints are blocked too. Googlebot therefore renders an unstyled page, which hurts mobile-usability and layout signals. There is also no 'Sitemap:' directive.

Fix: Replace with a minimal robots.txt: 'User-agent: *', 'Disallow: /wp-admin/', 'Allow: /wp-admin/admin-ajax.php', and targeted disallows for '/*?add-to-cart=', '/*?orderby=', '/*?filter_', '/*?s=', '/cart/', '/checkout/', '/my-account/'. Add 'Sitemap: https://lespressousa.com/wp-sitemap.xml' (or the SEO plugin's sitemap). Do not block /wp-content/ or /wp-includes/.

HighA11Y-01

Sitewide color-contrast failures

Evidence and fix

axe color-contrast failures (serious): home 18 nodes, shop 61, category 102, PDP 54, cart 17, checkout 27, contact 14, business solutions 22, more-information 33, franchise 29, where-to-buy 25. The main nav is #808080 on #fff (3.94:1) at 13px. Sidebar and filter links are light grey. Product-card 'Add to cart' and 'Show Details' buttons are near-invisible pale grey. The 'View cart' button on the add-to-cart notice is white on pale green. Links in body text are only distinguishable by color (1.69:1 against #666 text, 9 link-in-text-block violations).

Fix: Set the minimum body/nav text to #595959 or darker (7:1 on white), make primary buttons #C2410C-like orange with white text at 4.5:1 or higher, and underline links in body copy.

HighA11Y-02

No visible keyboard focus, no skip link, 42 header tab stops

Evidence and fix

Computed :focus styles on nav links, buttons and inputs are 'outline: none' with no box-shadow or color change. There is no skip link. The header contains 42 focusable elements, so a keyboard user tabs through every mega-menu link before reaching content. Dropdowns open on focus, but adjacent submenus (About and Join Us) stay open and overlap translucently.

Fix: Add a global :focus-visible outline (2px, offset 2px, at least 3:1 contrast) and a 'Skip to content' link. Use disclosure-pattern menus (button with aria-expanded) so submenus open only on Enter or Space and close on Esc or blur.

HighA11Y-03

Form fields without programmatic labels (all Contact Form 7 forms)

Evidence and fix

axe 'label' (critical): /contact/ 4 of 4 fields, /join-us/more-information/ 12 fields, /join-us/franchise-info-request/ 15 fields, plus select-name violations (1-3 per form). The visible text 'Your Name (required)' is not tied to the input. Required state is shown only as text in the label. The footer category <select> and the select2 widget have no accessible name.

Fix: Wrap each CF7 field in <label> (or use for/id), add autocomplete attributes (name, email, tel, postal-code, organization), mark required fields with aria-required and a visible asterisk legend, and add aria-label to the footer category select.

HighB2B-01

Business inquiry form is long and has no fallback

Evidence and fix

/join-us/more-information/ ('Business Solutions Inquiry'): 14 fields, 9 required (name, email, phone, position, city, ZIP, company type, current coffee system, 'what can we help you with'), plus Turnstile and a generic 'Send Request' button. Only 3 labels say '(required)': position, city and ZIP are enforced without being marked. There is no promise of response time, no phone or email alternative on the page, and no privacy note. The 'Inquire about customized solutions' link on Business Solutions is small orange inline text.

Fix: Step 1 (4 fields: name, business email, business type, ZIP), then optional step 2 (volume, current system, timeline). Add click-to-call and 'We reply within 1 business day', and track form submissions in GA4.

HighB2B-02

Franchise form asks for too much too early

Evidence and fix

/join-us/franchise-info-request/: 13 fields, 9 required, including full street address (2 lines), state, ZIP and two required essay textareas ('plans, aspirations and long-term goals', 'Why would Guglielmo USA select you'). It asks yes/no about a $2.5 million net worth and $1 million liquid assets with no context, fees or FDD information. The Join Us page promises 'Over 35 locations worldwide' and 'Advertising reimbursement*' with no footnote.

Fix: Publish a franchise overview (investment range, support, unit economics, FDD request) and a short qualifying form (name, email, phone, state, capital range dropdown). Move the essays to a later call.

HighCHK-01

Shipping cost is invisible until a full address is entered

Evidence and fix

The cart has no shipping row and no shipping calculator ('Subtotal $32.50 / Total $32.50'). Checkout shows 'Total $32.50' with no shipping or tax line until the address is complete (UPS live rates). No free-shipping threshold or delivery estimate appears anywhere in cart or checkout, and there is no shipping policy page. Unexpected shipping cost is the top-cited cause of checkout abandonment (Baymard).

Fix: Enable the WooCommerce cart shipping calculator (ZIP only), show 'Shipping calculated at next step – from $X' or a flat-rate table, add a free-shipping progress bar, and publish a Shipping policy page linked from PDP, cart and footer.

HighCONT-01

Contradictory facts and claims the catalog cannot back

Evidence and fix

Blends: 'Eight distinct blends of espresso' (/espressoblends/) vs 'seven blends' (/join-us/business-solutions/foodservice-solutions/) vs 6 named blends plus 'Special Blends' in the menu. Heritage: '70 years' (/join-us/, office page) vs 'over 60 years' (/dripcoffee/) vs '70+ years' (story page). Service: 'over 25 years' (/join-us/, /equipment/) vs 'over 20 years' (/join-us/business-solutions/). Drip: /dripcoffee/ claims 'single origin, organic, flavored and cold brew blends', 'Six distinct blends', 'Two coffee roasts developed exclusively for the Cold Brew method' and 'locally roasted in small batches'. It shows 6 blend cards (Premium, Java Estate, Brazil Oro, Colombia, Decaf Colombia, French Roast), but the catalog sells only 3 drip blends (Premium, French Roast, Decaf) in 8 SKUs, 3 of them out of stock, and no cold brew. 'Locally roasted' conflicts with the imported-from-Italy positioning. About says 'five distributor's nation wide'; Where to Find lists 3.

Fix: Create a single fact sheet (founding years, number of blends, service years, distributor count) approved by the client. Remove unsellable drip and cold-brew claims or add the products. Separate the L'Espresso drip line from Guglielmo espresso in the IA.

HighHOME-01

No value proposition, product or CTA above the fold

Evidence and fix

Desktop fold (1440x900): a 137px blurred hero strip with the tagline image, a row of 5 icon links (Our Coffee, Machines, Join Us, Where to Buy, Learn More), then three columns titled 'GOING QUICKLY…', 'GUGLIELMO NEAR YOU' and 'JOIN OUR FAMILY'. There are no product photos of the blends, no 'Shop now' button and no statement of who Guglielmo is (Italian roaster, imported, Certified Espresso Italiano). On mobile, the first screen (390x844) holds only the 82px header, an 80px hero strip and 5 stacked icon links (~510px).

Fix: Hero with lifestyle and product imagery, an H1 value proposition (authentic Italian espresso from a family roaster), and 2 CTAs (Shop Coffee and Capsules, Solutions for Business). Follow with bestsellers, a 'find your blend' module, heritage proof points and a B2B band.

HighPDP-02

HighPERF-01

104+ CSS/JS files and 56-61 render-blocking resources per page

Evidence and fix

Every page loads about 50 stylesheets and 54-60 scripts. Chrome reports 56-61 resources as renderBlockingStatus='blocking' (home: 57, about 194 KB). By origin: Enfold 36, notice-bar plugin 7 (slick, tweecool, ticker CSS/JS for a bar that is not displayed), UPS shipping 2, Authorize.net CIM 2, Google Fonts 2, bootstrapcdn 1, CF7 1, bulk-discount 1. 8 synchronous scripts sit in <head>. Requests per page: home 130, shop 120, category 131, PDP 134, machine PDP 136, cart 121.

Load CF7 and Turnstile only on form pages, and Authorize.net/UPS/PayPal assets only on cart and checkout (Asset CleanUp or Perfmatters). Defer non-critical JS. Target under 40 requests and under 15 render-blocking.

/my-account/ shows Login and Register forms, each with a Cloudflare Turnstile widget and pale (disabled-looking) buttons, but no benefits (order history, quick reorder, subscriptions, wholesale pricing). It sits in the main nav as text rather than an icon.

Fix: Explain the benefits, add social or magic-link login, and plan B2B accounts with tier pricing and reorder in the redesign.

LowCONT-06

Naming and labeling inconsistencies

Evidence and fix

'Club 99 Capsules (50 Pack)' has the slug 'club-99-capsules-12-pack'. The page is called 'Where to Find' in the H1 and title, 'Where to Buy' in the URL and icon, and 'Distributors' in a legacy URL. 'Partner With Guglielmo USA' (menu) is 'Request More Information' (H1). 'Franchise Info request' uses inconsistent casing. 'puly CAFF plus®' vs 'Puly Milk Plus®'. 'ROCKET APPARTAMENTO TCA (CHROME/BLACK) + ESPRESSO' is in all caps.

Fix: Adopt a product naming convention (Blend – Format – Size, e.g. '5 Stelle Whole Bean Espresso, 1 kg') and a page naming glossary.

LowNAV-05

Footer offers little utility

Evidence and fix

The footer has a 'Select a category' dropdown, a sitemap list that duplicates the header, no address, phone, email, hours, social links, newsletter or payment and trust badges, '© Copyright Guglielmo USA -' (no year, dangling dash), and a 'powered by Enfold WordPress Theme' link on 186 pages.

Fix: Rebuild the footer with contact details, policies (Shipping, Returns, Privacy, Terms), a B2B contact, newsletter signup and social links. Remove the theme credit.

LowPDP-06

Related products and cross-sells are generic

Evidence and fix

Every PDP shows 4 random 'Related products' from the same category (a 5 Stelle 1 kg page shows $105-110 drip bulk bags). There are no upsells (e.g. 1 kg to a case), no cross-sells (capsules with a machine, cups with coffee) and no 'Frequently bought together'. The cart has 0 cross-sells.

Fix: Set curated upsells and cross-sells per product (capsule-machine compatibility, blend to the same blend in other formats) and add 'Pairs with' and 'Complete your setup' modules.

LowPERF-07

Cart/checkout TTFB and DOM size

Evidence and fix

Uncached cart TTFB is 784 ms on desktop and 1.19s on the throttled run; other pages are 100-230 ms (Cloudflare and WP Engine cache HITs). The DOM is 572-1,122 nodes. HTML carries 22 KB of inline CSS and 15-27 KB of inline JS on every page.

Fix: Audit the plugins that hook into cart totals (bulk discount, UPS live rates) and enable an object cache (Redis on WP Engine).

LowSEO-09

Favicon, PWA and security headers

Evidence and fix

Only a 23x23 PNG favicon (2014) is declared. /favicon.ico and /apple-touch-icon.png return HTTP 200 with 0 bytes (empty files). There is no web manifest (/manifest.json 404) and no theme-color. No HSTS, X-Content-Type-Options, Referrer-Policy or CSP headers were observed. http://www redirects in 2 hops (http://www, then https://www, then the https apex).

Fix: Upload a 512x512 Site Icon (WordPress generates all sizes), add a manifest and theme-color, enable HSTS and basic security headers at Cloudflare, and collapse the www redirect into 1 hop.

LowSEO-10

Aggressive bot rate limiting

Evidence and fix

During a polite crawl (~1 request/s) Cloudflare returned 429 'Just a moment…' challenge pages (noindex,nofollow) for 5 URLs. Verified search bots are normally exempt, but SEO tools, uptime monitors and link-preview fetchers may be challenged.

Fix: Review Cloudflare Bot Fight Mode and rate-limit rules. Allow-list verified bots and set a rate-limit threshold above normal crawl rates.

LowTRUST-04

Social links are legacy and off-brand for the US

Evidence and fix

Social icons appear only in the content-page sidebar, not in the footer. Facebook links to the Italian parent page (it-it.facebook.com/CaffeGuglielmoOfficialFanpage). Twitter (http://twitter.com/CaffeGuglielmo) and Pinterest use http. YouTube points to a channel /feed URL. There is no Instagram, the most relevant channel for coffee. Sidebar social links are inside an aria-hidden container.

Fix: Confirm the US-owned or parent channels with the client, link them over https in the footer, add Instagram, and embed UGC or Instagram content on home.

Redesign

What only a redesign fixes.

  1. Dual-audience information architecture. Separate, clearly signposted B2C shop and B2B hub (segments, equipment and service, wholesale accounts, distributors, franchise) instead of 'Join Us'. Patching menus cannot fix this. (NAV-01, B2B-01..05)

Blends, formats and sizes are modelled 3 times (categories, tags and 9 pa_* attribute taxonomies). 87 mostly simple products, 46% out of stock, retail and wholesale SKUs mixed, quote-only machines at $0.

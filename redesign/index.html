01 · Where the site stands

A great coffee on a 2014 website.

The product is distinctive, certified and loved by the people who find it. The site makes it hard to find, hard to choose and hard to trust, and it hides the business offer behind "Join Us".

40/86

Homepage today: 130 requests · 1.0 MB · LCP 2.8 s.

Brand & Storytelling

3/10

70+ years of Italian roasting heritage is buried: the hero is a 137px strip using a 284x300 thumbnail stretched to 1440px with tagline-as-image, About is 3 paragraphs about 'L'Espresso USA', and 'The Story of Guglielmo' has 44 words plus an outbound link.

Visual Design

3/10

Stock 2014 Enfold template: grey 13px text, faint ghost buttons, mixed icon styles, 2014-15 product photos (75 of 156 catalog images) and inconsistent card layouts. It reads as dated and generic, not premium Italian.

Navigation & IA

4/10

The Shop mega-menu is usable, but B2B sits behind the vague label 'Join Us'. 'About' is not clickable, 'Espresso Blends' sits under About, Learn More anchors are wrong, Equipment pages are not in the menu, and the mobile menu is a flat list of 38 links.

Product Discovery

3/10

/shop/ shows only 3 tiles, filters are single-select link lists that sit 7,762px down on mobile, 46% of SKUs are out of stock with no label, and search returns blog-style results with no images or prices.

Product Detail Pages

4/10

The buy box is clean and the case-discount table is a real asset. But the median is 1 image and 54 words of description, there are no reviews or shipping/returns info, an internal 'Equipment Test' option is exposed to buyers, and 'Call for Price' pages have no quote CTA.

Cart & Checkout

5/10

Guest checkout plus PayPal/Venmo/Google Pay on a single page is solid. However, shipping cost is invisible until a full address is entered, 'Apply coupon' is the dominant button in the cart, and there is no express pay on the cart or PDP.

B2B Lead Generation

3/10

Leads go through a 14-field form (9 required) or a 13-field franchise form (9 required, $2.5M net-worth question). There is no phone on the B2B or Contact pages, the distributor path is a PDF, the Distributors tab is empty, and the pages contradict each other.

Mobile

3/10

On 3G with 4x CPU throttling, LCP is 7.2-13.8s and CLS is 0.33 on home. 73% of tap targets on home are under 24px, the median font is 13px, filters sit below the products, and the PDP buy box falls below the fold for returning visitors.

Performance

4/10

Desktop is acceptable (LCP 1.4-2.8s), but every page makes 120-136 requests (~50 CSS + ~55 JS, 56-61 render-blocking). Images are already served as WebP by content negotiation; GTM, Smartlook and Turnstile load sitewide.

Accessibility

3/10

Every audited page has critical axe violations. Contrast failures range from 14 to 102 nodes per page. There is no visible focus indicator, no skip link and 42 header tab stops; CF7 forms have up to 15 unlabeled fields, and 63% of images lack alt text.

SEO

2/10

/shop/ and all 18 categories, 5 tags and 11 brands are noindex (Enfold default; no SEO plugin). robots.txt blocks 52 of 54 CSS/JS files. There are 0 meta descriptions and 0 OG tags, about 41% of sitemap URLs cannot be indexed, and the homepage H1 is 'Front Page'.

Content Quality

3/10

15+ typos and contradictory facts (60 vs 70 years, 20 vs 25 years, 7 vs 8 blends, 6 drip blends claimed vs 3 sold). Also: 2 broken shortcodes, invisible white text, health claims from 2006-2015 and 7 password-protected products published.

Trust & Social Proof

2/10

0 reviews across 87 products and only 2 undated testimonials. No phone, email or address in the header, footer or Contact page; no shipping policy; '© Copyright Guglielmo USA -' with no year. Social links point to the Italian parent page.

03 · What every concept fixes

Eight principles, in all five designs.

  • Two clear doors. A For Home | For Business switch in the header, each with its own navigation and main action.
  • Shop by your machine. 36 mm capsules, Espresso 70 (Nespresso®-compatible), ESE pods, beans and ground: compatibility on every product.
  • One intensity scale. 1 to 10 for every blend, with roast, composition, tasting notes and the best way to brew it.
  • No dead ends. Out-of-stock items get "Notify me", quote-only machines say "Request a quote", and the public "Order Adjustment" item goes.
  • Visible value. Case discounts, subscribe & save, free-shipping progress and honest cost-per-cup maths, shown where people decide.
  • Short forms first. Six fields or fewer for a first contact, a tasting offer on every business page.
  • One source of facts. Founded 1943, Copanello roastery since 1972, L'Espresso USA since 2000: the same everywhere.
  • Fast and accessible. A real performance budget, WCAG 2.2 AA, alt text, meta descriptions and structured data on every template.

CONCEPT 01

Centro

Italian pop modernism, built on the bullseye.

Guglielmo owns one of the most graphic marks in coffee. Centro turns the bullseye and "il caffè che fa centro" into a full brand system: concentric rings, flat brand primaries, poster-scale type and a coffee finder that literally hits the mark.

Built for: Home espresso drinkers, capsule owners, gift buyers, new customers

  • "Hit your mark" coffee-finder quiz
  • Intensity rings on every product
  • Subscribe & save on every coffee
  • Blend explorer by packaging colour

Motion: Snappy, springy, poster-like: rings rotate into place, a marquee of blend names, a bullseye ripple when the quiz lands.

CONCEPT 02

Calabria

Editorial heritage, told like a magazine.

A roaster founded in Catanzaro in 1943 and roasting on the Ionian coast since 1972 deserves to be told like a magazine. Calabria positions Guglielmo next to illy: slow photographic chapters, a Tasting Room for every blend, and commerce placed inside the stories.

Built for: Discerning home buyers, Italian-American loyalists, press and partners

  • Tasting Room with sensory profiles and blend comparison
  • Il Giornale magazine and long-form article
  • Scroll-drawn 1943 to today timeline
  • Shoppable "blend in this story" modules

Motion: Slow tide: shutter reveals on photographs, gentle parallax, headlines that rise word by word, a timeline scrubbed by scroll.

CONCEPT 03

Pro

Guglielmo Professional, business first.

Wholesale, equipment and service for New England businesses, plus new distributors, are the growth engine. Pro leads with outcomes and makes the tasting, proposal and reorder flows effortless, with real cost-per-cup and margin maths. The consumer shop stays one click away.

Built for: Café, restaurant and pastry owners, office managers, hotels, distributors

  • 4-step Solution Finder with cost per cup, margin and lease estimate
  • Trade portal: reorder with PO, invoices, equipment, tickets
  • Service hub with triaged requests and maintenance checklists
  • Partner programmes: distributor, retailer, Guglielmo Point

Motion: Precise instrument: short, exact transitions; numbers that tween as inputs change; wizard steps that slide; no decoration.

CONCEPT 04

Crema

A dark, cinematic showroom for machines and systems.

Three incompatible single-serve systems confuse buyers today. Crema makes the choice effortless and the ritual desirable with cinematic product storytelling, then sells the machine and coffee together on an honest cost per shot.

Built for: Machine buyers, capsule and pod owners, gift buyers

  • "Choose your system" comparison and recommender
  • Bundle builder with live cost per shot and yearly savings
  • Generative crema hero and scroll-scrubbed product scenes
  • Machine pages with spec callouts and compatible coffees

Motion: Cinematic: a generative crema swirl, sticky product scenes scrubbed by scroll, cards that re-order with spring physics.

CONCEPT 05

Quartiere

Boston's Italian coffee family, community first.

A family-run Boston importer whose Calabrian coffee is poured from the North End to Belmont, delivered in three business days and serviced in person. Quartiere builds the brand around people and places, makes finding Guglielmo central, and keeps loyal customers with a passport and referrals.

Built for: Greater Boston locals, the Italian-American community nationwide, local venues

  • Il Quartiere: map-led venue stories
  • Il Passaporto loyalty stamp card and referral
  • Italian coffee recipes with the right blend
  • Locator with ZIP search and distributor map

Motion: Friendly bounce: map pins drop with springs, stamps thunk onto the card, postcards tilt, awnings drift.

06 · Roadmap

From demo to WordPress in about twenty weeks.

The chosen direction becomes a custom block theme on your existing WooCommerce store, replacing Enfold. Quick wins on today's site can start this week.

Week 1

Quick wins on today's site

Remove "Order Adjustment", fix the broken newsletter and referral pages, add meta descriptions, alt text and contact details, hide dead out-of-stock items, correct typos and conflicting claims.

Weeks 2–8

Foundation

Catalog restructure (variable products per blend, system and intensity attributes), block theme in the chosen direction, Home, Shop, Product, Cart and Checkout blocks, express payments, redirects, SEO.

Weeks 9–14

Growth

Subscriptions, bundles, the quiz or system chooser, reviews programme, email flows, business landing and solution finder wired to a CRM.

Weeks 15–20

Trade and community

Trade accounts and quick reorder, service booking, loyalty and referral, journal and recipes, venue map.

07 · Decisions for you

What we need from you.

  1. Which direction (or which mix: for example Centro's brand system with Pro's business tooling and Crema's system chooser).
  2. The public business address: Guglielmo's distributor list shows 266 Calvary St, Waltham, MA 02453; the site says Boston.
  3. Heritage claims to use everywhere: founded 1943 by Guglielmo Papaleo in Catanzaro; roastery at Copanello since 1972.
  4. Commercial policies shown in the demos: 10% subscribe & save, free shipping at $59, a tasting kit, "Give $10, get $10" referral.
  5. Proposed intensity scores and tasting notes for each blend (Guglielmo publishes none) and the Extra Bar composition conflict.
  6. Photography: a press kit from Guglielmo SpA, high-resolution packshots, and a local shoot with partner venues in Boston.

Support

Find a fix below, or get in touch.

Something isn't skipping

Nothing happens on Netflix or Prime Video

Open the CleanCut popup and check that a rating other than Off is selected. Then check the status line. If it says subtitles not scanned yet, turn on English captions once so the player downloads the subtitle file. Reload the page after installing or updating the extension.

A scene played that shouldn't have

Some scenes have no tag and no revealing subtitle line, so there's nothing to act on. You can tag it yourself: press Alt+[ where it starts and Alt+] where it ends, then pick a category. If you're signed in, share the tag so everyone benefits.

Skips land a bit early or late (mostly Prime Video)

Prime Video's clock can differ from the programme time, especially with ad breaks. Use the Sync nudge in the popup to shift timing for that title.

It worked yesterday and doesn't today

Streaming sites change their pages without notice, which can break detection until we update. Please report it with the details below. We prioritise fixes for Netflix and Prime Video.

I can't reach the community server

CleanCut still works from your own tags and the subtitle scan. Check your connection, and in Options confirm the server address is the default.

Report a problem

Email [[SUPPORT_EMAIL]] or open an issue at [[ISSUES_URL]]. Please include:

  • Your browser and version, and the CleanCut version (shown in chrome://extensions).
  • The site and the title/episode, and what you expected to happen.
  • Whether the popup showed community tags, scan results or an error.

Please don't send subtitle files or account passwords. We never need them.

Delete my data

Uninstalling removes everything on your device. To delete your community account and its tags, email us from the address you contacted us with and tell us your display name.
